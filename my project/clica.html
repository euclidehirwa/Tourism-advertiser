Welcome to Rwanda

A small country with extraordinary experiences

Rwanda is known as the Land of a Thousand Hills. It is famous for its beautiful green landscapes, mountain gorillas, national parks, lakes, wildlife and welcoming people.


From the mountains of Volcanoes National Park to the savannah of Akagera and the ancient rainforest of Nyungwe, Rwanda offers many different experiences for visitors.

Top Tourist Attractions

Explore some of the amazing places Rwanda has to offer

🦍 Volcanoes National Park

Located in northwestern Rwanda, Volcanoes National Park is famous for mountain gorilla trekking and its volcanic mountains.

👀 What can you see?

Mountain gorillas, golden monkeys, bamboo forests, birds and beautiful volcanic mountains.

🚗 How can you reach it?

You can travel from Kigali by road toward Musanze and then continue to the Volcanoes National Park area.

🦁 Akagera National Park

Akagera is Rwanda's savannah safari destination in the eastern part of the country.

👀 What can you see?

Lions, elephants, buffaloes, rhinos, giraffes, zebras, hippos, crocodiles and many bird species.

🚗 How can you reach it?

You can travel by road from Kigali toward the eastern part of Rwanda. The journey takes several hours depending on the route and conditions.

🌳 Nyungwe National Park

Nyungwe is one of Africa's oldest rainforests and is famous for its incredible biodiversity.

👀 What can you see?

Chimpanzees, monkeys, colorful birds, butterflies, orchids and beautiful rainforest scenery.

🚗 How can you reach it?

You can travel from Kigali toward southwestern Rwanda. Rusizi is one of the nearby towns used when exploring the park.

🌊 Lake Kivu

Lake Kivu is one of Africa's Great Lakes and is surrounded by beautiful hills, beaches and towns.

👀 What can you see?

Beautiful lake scenery, islands, mountains, beaches and spectacular sunsets.

🚗 How can you reach it?

You can travel west from Kigali toward towns such as Rubavu or Karongi.

🏙️ Kigali City

Kigali is Rwanda's capital city and an excellent starting point for exploring the country.

👀 What can you see?

Museums, markets, art galleries, restaurants, modern buildings and beautiful city views.

🚗 How can you reach it?

Kigali is the main entry point for many international visitors through Kigali International Airport.

How Can You Reach Rwanda's Attractions?

Rwanda is relatively easy to explore by road.

✈️

1. Arrive in Kigali

International visitors can arrive through Kigali International Airport, which provides access to the country's main tourism circuit.

🚗

2. Travel by Road

Rwanda's major tourist destinations can be connected by road. Cars, tour vehicles and other transport options can be used depending on the destination.

🗺️

3. Plan Your Route

A popular tourism circuit can include Kigali, Akagera National Park, Lake Kivu, Nyungwe National Park and Volcanoes National Park.

🧭

4. Use a Guide

For activities such as wildlife viewing and gorilla trekking, visitors should use authorized guides and follow park rules.
